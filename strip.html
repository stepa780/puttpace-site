Opened from the app? Your own lengths are already filled in.
  1. Print at 100% / Actual size (not "Fit to page"), landscape, on A4 or Letter.
  2. Check the black bar measures exactly 10 cm.
  3. Cut out the three strips. Tape each grey tab under the start of the next strip.
  4. Lay the strip beside the ball, with the ball circle level with the ball. Swing back and through to the marks for your distance.
This bar should measure exactly 10 cm. If not, print again at 100%.
