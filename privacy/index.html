PuttPace Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

PuttPace is a putting tempo trainer published by Sunday Studio Apps. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information you enter

Your settings (tempo, stroke length, line style, sounds, screen calibration and presets) are stored only on your device. PuttPace has no accounts, and we do not receive or store this information.

Advertising

The free version of PuttPace shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time under Ad privacy choices in the app's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Purchases

If you buy Remove ads, the payment is handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that the purchase was made, but never your payment details. After purchase, the app shows no ads and the advertising SDK is not started.

Children

PuttPace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
